# AUTO-PREMIUM fictional motor policy

Policy number: AUTO-PREMIUM. Effective for the fictional demonstration claims only.

Coverage: Accidental collision damage to the insured vehicle is covered, subject to submitted accident statement and repair estimate.

Coverage: Weather-related damage to the insured vehicle is covered, subject to submitted accident statement and repair estimate.

Deductible: 250 currency units per covered claim.

Exclusion: Deliberate damage by the insured is not covered.
