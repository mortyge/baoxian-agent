# AUTO-BASIC fictional motor policy

Policy number: AUTO-BASIC. Effective for the fictional demonstration claims only.

Coverage: Accidental collision damage to the insured vehicle is covered, subject to submitted accident statement and repair estimate.

Deductible: 500 currency units per collision claim.

Exclusion: Theft of personal belongings left inside the vehicle is not covered.

Exclusion: Mechanical breakdown without an accidental collision is not covered.

Exclusion: Flood water damage to the insured vehicle is not covered.
